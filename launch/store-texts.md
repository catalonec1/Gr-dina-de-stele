## Descriere scurtă (max ~120 caractere)
- EN: Plant stars, gather light and build glowing constellations. A relaxing idle game that grows even while you're away.
- RO: Plantează stele, strânge lumină și construiește constelații. Un joc idle relaxant care crește și când lipsești.
- RU: Сажай звёзды, собирай свет и создавай сияющие созвездия. Спокойная idle-игра, которая растёт, даже когда тебя нет.

## Descriere lungă (EN)
Tap the night sky to gather light, then plant stars that shine for you.
- 6 kinds of stars, from tiny Sparks to Supernovas
- Complete constellations to double a star's light
- Catch passing comets for big bonuses
- Daily gift with a 7-day streak and 3 new quests every day
- Cosmic Rebirth: reset for stardust and a permanent boost
- Earns light while you're offline
- English, Română, Русский · works on phone and desktop

## Controale
- EN: Tap or click the sky to gather light. Tap a comet to catch it. Use the bottom panel to buy stars and upgrades.
