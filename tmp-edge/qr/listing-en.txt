QR Pocket makes QR codes right in your browser. It works offline and collects no data: nothing you type is sent anywhere.

WHAT YOU CAN ENCODE
- Links and any text, with a live byte count
- Wi-Fi networks (WPA/WPA2/WPA3, WEP, open, hidden): guests scan and join without typing the password
- Contact cards (vCard 3.0): name, organization, job title, phones, e-mail, website, city, country
- E-mail with subject and message, SMS with a pre-filled text, phone calls

OPTIONS
- Error correction level L, M, Q or H
- PNG size 256 to 2048 px, quiet zone of 1, 2 or 4 modules
- Foreground and background colors, with a warning when contrast is too low or the code is inverted
- Shows the QR version and module count, and warns when a code gets too dense for printing

EXPORT
- Download PNG or SVG (vector, sharp at any print size)
- Copy the image to the clipboard
- Recent codes you downloaded or copied are kept in this browser only and can be cleared at any time; Wi-Fi passwords are not saved in the form

MORE
- Right-click selected text → "Make QR code for '…'"
- Shortcut Alt+Shift+Q, full-tab mode, light and dark theme

Free, no ads, no tracking.
