Star Garden – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Star Garden is a single-player idle game. It does not ask for, collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress (light, stars, upgrades, language and sound settings) is saved only in your browser's local storage on your own device. You can delete it at any time by clearing the site data or uninstalling the app.

Network access

The app loads its own files from GitHub Pages (catalonec1.github.io). GitHub may keep standard server logs as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. The Microsoft Store version shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/catalonec1/Gr-dina-de-stele.
