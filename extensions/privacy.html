Privacy Policy – JSON Pocket, Text Pocket, Unit Pocket

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Summary

These browser extensions work only inside your browser. They have no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no network requests. Nothing you type, paste or select ever leaves your computer.

Data processed on your device

  • Text you paste into the extension, or select on a page and send with the right-click menu, is processed locally and is never transmitted.
  • Your last input and the selected tab are kept in the extension's own local storage, so they are still there when you reopen it.
  • Right-click hand-off: the selected text is placed in temporary session storage (storage.session) for a few seconds, until the extension window reads it. It is cleared when the browser closes.

Permissions

  • contextMenus – adds the right-click menu items.
  • storage – the temporary right-click hand-off described above.

The extensions request no host permissions and cannot read the pages you visit.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or transfer any user data.
  • We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extensions' purpose.
  • We do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling an extension deletes everything it stored.
