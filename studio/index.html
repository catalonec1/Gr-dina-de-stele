Catalonec

Independent developer of pixel art assets and small games.

What I make

  • 16x16 pixel art asset packs: dungeon, winter, underwater, farming and sci-fi themes, with tiles, props and animated characters in one shared palette.
  • Chiptune sound effects and short music loops for retro games.
  • Small browser games, playable for free on this site.
  • Developer tools for Oracle SQL and PL/SQL.

How the assets are made

Sprites and sounds are generated procedurally by code. The generator code was written with the help of an AI assistant; no AI image or audio model is used. Every pack is checked by hand, sliced for game engines (Point filter, 16 pixels per unit) and ships with a demo scene.
