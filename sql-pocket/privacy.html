SQL Pocket – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Summary

SQL Pocket processes the text you paste or select only inside your browser. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The only data that ever leaves your computer is a Pro license key, and only when you choose to activate one.

Data processed on your device

  • SQL and other text you paste into the extension or select on a page and send with the right-click menu. It is transformed locally and is never transmitted.
  • Settings and snippets are stored with the browser's extension storage (storage.local) on your device.
  • Pro license status (the key, an activation id, the status and the date of the last check) is stored with storage.sync, so the browser may sync it between your own signed-in browsers.

Data sent to third parties

When you click Activate, and about once a week after that, the extension sends your license key, the activation id and an instance name ("SQL Pocket Chrome" or "SQL Pocket Firefox") to the license service of the shop where you bought Pro: Lemon Squeezy (api.lemonsqueezy.com) or Gumroad (api.gumroad.com). Their own privacy policies apply to that request and to your purchase. Nothing else is sent.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, except the license check described above.
  • We do not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to the extension's single purpose.
  • We do not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

Deleting your data

Use Deactivate on the Pro tab to remove the license key, or uninstall the extension to delete all of its stored data.

Contact

Questions: the support e-mail shown on the extension's store page.
